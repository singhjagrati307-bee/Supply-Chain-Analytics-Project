# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **End-to-End Supply Chain Analytics Solution** designed to transform raw operational data into meaningful business insights using **Python, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI**.

The project simulates a real-world Business Intelligence workflow followed by analytics teams to analyze business performance, identify operational bottlenecks, monitor Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), and support strategic decision-making.

The complete analytics pipeline includes:

- 📥 Data Loading
- 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
- 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- 🗄️ SQLite Database Creation
- 💻 SQL Business Analysis
- 📈 KPI Development
- 📉 Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- 💡 Executive Business Insights

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Supply chain organizations generate massive amounts of transactional and operational data every day.

However, raw data alone does not provide meaningful business value.

Business stakeholders require answers to critical questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most to sales?
- Which product categories drive business growth?
- Are customer orders delivered on time?
- Which cities perform best?
- What operational challenges reduce delivery performance?
- How can the company improve overall supply chain efficiency?

This project addresses these questions through a complete Business Intelligence workflow.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Measure business KPIs.
- Identify top-performing customers.
- Evaluate product performance.
- Analyze category-wise revenue.
- Monitor delivery performance using OTIF metrics.
- Discover operational bottlenecks.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several valuable business insights that can help management improve revenue, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency.

---

## 🥛 1. Dairy Category Dominates Revenue

- Dairy products contribute the highest share of total revenue.
- More than half of the company's revenue comes from dairy products.
- Butter and Milk products consistently outperform other categories.

### Business Impact

- Maintain adequate inventory levels for dairy products.
- Prioritize production planning for high-demand dairy items.
- Continue promotional campaigns for best-selling dairy products.

---

## 🛒 2. Revenue is Concentrated Among a Few Customers

The customer analysis shows that a small number of customers contribute a significant portion of total revenue.

### Business Impact

- Strengthen relationships with top customers.
- Introduce loyalty programs.
- Reduce dependency by acquiring new high-value customers.

---

## 📦 3. Best-Selling Products Drive Business Growth

A limited number of products generate a major share of business revenue.

Top-performing products include:

- AM Butter 500
- AM Milk 500
- AM Butter 250
- AM Milk 250
- AM Curd 500

### Business Impact

- Ensure continuous stock availability.
- Improve demand forecasting.
- Reduce stock-out situations.

---

## 🚚 4. Delivery Performance Needs Improvement

OTIF performance indicates that a considerable number of customer orders are not delivered completely on time.

Late deliveries reduce customer satisfaction and increase operational costs.

### Business Impact

- Improve warehouse coordination.
- Optimize transportation planning.
- Monitor OTIF on a regular basis.
- Reduce order fulfillment delays.

---

## 📈 5. Monthly Revenue Trends Support Better Planning

Monthly revenue analysis highlights seasonal demand variations.

### Business Impact

- Improve production planning.
- Optimize inventory levels.
- Forecast future demand more accurately.

---

## 🏙️ 6. City-Wise Performance Analysis

Business performance differs across cities.

Understanding regional demand helps improve inventory allocation and distribution planning.

### Business Impact

- Optimize warehouse allocation.
- Improve city-specific marketing strategies.
- Increase inventory availability in high-demand regions.
